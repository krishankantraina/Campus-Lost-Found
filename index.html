Simple Process

How CampusFind Works

A streamlined, secure 3-step workflow designed specifically for our university campus

1

Report Your Item

Lost an item or found something unattended? Create a quick report specifying the item category, campus location, and description.

2

Browse & Connect

Filter through active listings. Use our integrated contact button to safely reach out to the finder or loser using verified student emails.

3

Recover Belongings

Meet at an authorized campus location like the Library Desk or Campus Security Office to verify ownership and reclaim your item safely.

Campus Safe Exchange Zone For safety and security, always meet during daylight hours at designated campus safe exchange locations such as the Central Library Reception or the Campus Police Desk (Student Center Rm 102).
About The Initiative

Empowering Our University with a Smarter Lost & Found

CampusFind was created to eliminate bulletin-board clutter and fragmented social media posts. Every year, hundreds of valuable textbooks, calculators, student IDs, and keys go unclaimed simply because there is no unified platform.

Fast & Direct Communication

Connect directly with students and campus departments with zero red tape and instant alerts.

Campus Community Driven

Built specifically for students, teaching assistants, faculty, and campus maintenance staff.

Transparent & Accountable

Track item recovery status from report submission all the way to return confirmation.
